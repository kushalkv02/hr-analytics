Overtime is the largest gap in this sample. Attrition is 30.53% with overtime and 10.44% without (about 2.9 times), and the gap shows up within every tenure band, age band, travel level and department. Choose “Works overtime” and compare tenure to see it.

Attrition rate = leavers ÷ employees in the segment. This is a snapshot of a public sample, not annualized turnover. Associations shown here do not explain why anyone left, one-variable splits are not statistically adjusted, and groups under 30 employees are greyed because their rates are unstable. Figures are aggregate SQL results only; the dashboard holds no employee-level data.
